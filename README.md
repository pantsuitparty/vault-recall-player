# Vault Recall player

An offline flashcard player for decks made in Vault Recall. Add it to your iPhone Home Screen and it works with no internet: Review, Quiz, Match and Timeline, with your pictures.

- **Getting decks in:** in Vault Recall, tap **Copy for phone**. In the player, tap **Paste from Vault Recall**.
- **Getting progress back:** in the player, tap **Copy progress**. In Vault Recall, tap the paste box under Offline phone player and paste.

Decks and progress stay on the phone (in the browser's storage for this site). Nothing is uploaded. This repository only holds the app itself.

When you change `index.html`, bump `VERSION` in `sw.js` so installed copies pick up the update.
